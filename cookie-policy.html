Cookie Policy

Last updated: June 2025

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences and improve your experience.

Cookies we use

Essential cookies: Modus Revise itself does not set any cookies that are required for the site to function. Your flashcard progress and exam practice progress are stored locally in your browser only and are not sent to any server.

Advertising cookies: We use Google AdSense, which may set cookies on your device to deliver personalised advertisements. These are third-party cookies controlled by Google.

Managing cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of some parts of the site. To opt out of Google's personalised advertising cookies, visit Google's Ads Settings.
