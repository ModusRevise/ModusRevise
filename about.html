Our Story

About Modus Revise

Modus Revise was built by students, for students. We know how stressful GCSE revision can be — and we know that most revision resources are either too expensive, too boring, or too generic to actually help.

Our goal is simple: give every student access to high-quality, free revision materials that actually work. No filler, no fluff — just the content you need, laid out in a way that makes sense.

What we cover

Right now Modus Revise covers Macbeth (AQA GCSE) in full — plot summary, context, characters, themes, technical terms, key quotes, quote analysis, flashcards, real past paper exam practice, and exam tips. We're actively working on expanding to other GCSE texts and subjects.
